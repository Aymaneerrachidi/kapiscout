KAPISCOUT BOT - FEATURE LIST
(Robinhood Chain scout bot on Telegram)

Paste a contract address and the bot scans it automatically - no command
required. The first scan on a token permanently records the caller, entry
market cap and proof receipt. Prefix a contract with "." to ignore it.


RESEARCH
  /scan            Full token card: price, market cap, liquidity, holders,
                   buys/sells, pair age, chart (runs automatically when you
                   paste a contract address)
  /chart           Price chart for a token
  /holders         Top holders breakdown
  /holderchanges   Recent holder count changes
  /pnl             Profit / loss view for a token
  /intel           Reality-check intelligence on a token
  /quote           Quick quote / exit quote for a token
  /devhistory      Deployer (dev) wallet history - see if the dev is dumping
  /timeline        Token timeline / history
  /digest          Daily digest

CALLS & SOCIAL PROOF
  /calls           Call history
  /active          Active plays right now
  /lb              Leaderboard of the best callers (trade records)
  /reallb          Real alpha - wins minus losses, no hype
  /callers         Who calls what - caller stats
  /stats           Overall bot stats
  /summary         Group card summary of a token

WALLET TRACKING
  /addwallet       0x... Name      Add a wallet to your watchlist
  /namewallet      0x... New Name  Rename a watched wallet
  /wallets                           List your watched wallets
  /removewallet                     Remove a wallet
  /portfolio                        Combined PnL of your watchlist
  /walletscore                      Score of a watched wallet
  /alert            Alerts on price / volume moves of watched wallets

PAPER TRADING (SIMULATOR)
  /paper           Paper trading menu - every user gets $100 automatically,
                   no admin setup needed
  /paperbuy        Buy a token with your paper balance (or use the Paper
                   Buy button on a scan card)
  /papersell       Sell a token you hold
  /paperlb         Leaderboard of the best paper traders

RADAR
  /bridgeflow      Robinhood Chain bridge flow
  /bridgealerts    Alerts for big bridge movements
  /bridgemin       Set the minimum bridge amount for alerts

SETTINGS
  /settings        Open your settings
  /showchart       Toggle chart display on scans
  /chartmode       Chart display mode
  /timeframe       Default chart timeframe
  /minmc           Minimum market cap threshold for scans (ignore rugs)

HELP
  /help            Show this list at any time
