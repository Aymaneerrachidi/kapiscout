Blockscout on Robinhood

A chain is only useful when people can read it.

Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Layer 2 built on Ethereum for onchain financial markets. Blockscout is its official block explorer from block one: the public window where transactions, contracts, holders, transfers, deployers, and verification status become visible.

For a memecoin community surrounded by noise and copycats, the explorer has one essential job. It lets the huddle check the exact contract before trusting the story.

KapiScout's story

Quiet feet.
Sharp eyes.

October 2024

Blockscout published the story behind its capybara mascot. The idea began at a company offsite in Dubai: combine the trust of a scout with the friendly calm of a capybara.

Capybaras gather in huddles. They adapt to land and water, stay composed under pressure, and warn their friends when danger is close. Those traits became a natural language for exploring blockchains together.

The first scouts

The character came to represent community, adaptability, helpfulness, honesty, and respect. Blockscout moved away from a realistic animal and chose a warm cartoon scout built for badges, stickers, and onchain adventures.

A new forest

KapiScout carries that explorer spirit into Robinhood Chain as a new community character. He is unofficial, independent, and never pretends to speak for Robinhood or Blockscout.

He peeks through the grass when bears are near, reads the trail before running, checks the contract twice, and remembers where every trail began.

The promise

When candles rise, he smiles quietly. When candles fall, he stays seated, steady, and curious. His power is not noise. His power is scouting.
