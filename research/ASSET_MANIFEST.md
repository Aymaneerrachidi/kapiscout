# Asset Manifest

Research date: 2026-08-14

The `assets` directory contains 180 raster images, 47 SVG files, three original ZIP archives, and 244 total files (about 50.78 MB). Web images were downloaded from their original Ghost storage paths when available; resized `w600`, `w1000`, and similar variants were normalized to the original asset URL.

### `assets/02_2024_retrospective`

Full raw image pull from Blockscout's 2024 retrospective. The most relevant files are:

- `Blockscout-Capybara-evolution-1-1.png` — visual evolution overview.

### `assets/05_brand_kits`

Official downloadable archives and their extracted contents:

Important: these are reference/brand assets, not proof of permission to use Blockscout branding in a commercial token.
