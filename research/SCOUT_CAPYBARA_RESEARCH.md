# KapiScout / Robinhood Chain Research

Research date: 2026-08-14

## Chosen project name

**KapiScout**

Collision check on 2026-08-14:

- Exact web searches for `KapiScout`, `Kapi Scout` with crypto/token terms, and `KAPISCOUT` did not find an existing crypto project using the exact name.
- Live Robinhood Chain Blockscout API searches for `kapiscout`, `kapi scout`, and `KAPISCOUT` returned zero token matches.
- This is a promising preliminary clearance, not a legal trademark clearance. Handles and domains still need to be checked immediately before registration.

Recommended written form: **KapiScout** (capital K and S). Use `kapiscout` for domains and handles. The project name is locked; the token ticker still needs a final choice. The cleanest candidates are `$KAPI` (short and memorable, but more collision-prone) and `$KSCOUT` (more distinctive and descriptive).

## Bottom line

The Blockscout Capybara is an official Blockscout mascot, not a Robinhood mascot. The strongest truthful connection to Robinhood Chain is that Blockscout operates the chain's official block explorer. A community memecoin can build a story around a capybara scout exploring Robinhood Chain, but it should not claim endorsement by Robinhood or Blockscout.

There is already heavy name collision on Robinhood Chain. A live Blockscout search for `capybara` returned dozens of themed tokens. At least four results use the `CAPYBARA` ticker, along with `RCAPY`, `RUNNER`, `CAPYBARAS`, `TREX`, and other variations. A distinctive name and ticker centered on the scout/explorer idea will be stronger than another generic `$CAPYBARA`.

## Canonical mascot lore

The following is directly supported by Blockscout's own material:

- The concept was born at a Blockscout company offsite in Dubai.
- The idea combines the trustworthiness of a scout with the friendliness of a capybara.
- The mascot represents community, adaptability, helpfulness, honesty, respect, and calmness under pressure.
- Capybaras gather in groups called huddles, adapt to land and water, and warn their group of danger. Blockscout used those traits as metaphors for working across projects, dapps, layers, and chains.
- The character's role is to guide people through blockchain exploration and help them earn digital merit badges.
- Early concepts were more realistic. Blockscout chose a cartoon style to make the character warmer, more playful, and more meme-friendly.
- The public debut was ETHGlobal Brussels 2024 on stickers and merchandise.
- The three debut variants were:
  - Friend Scout: a social character shown in a dab pose.
  - Hacker Scout: a hooded codebreaker.
  - Profi Scout: a trusted guide who knows the way.
- The mascot became the face of the Blockscout Merits and NFT badge program. Named examples include Fuel Up Hero, Swapscout Pioneer, Dapp Explorer, and Blockscout Fren.
- Blockscout used four rarity tiers for some Capybara NFT badges.
- A 170 cm capybara statue appeared at ETHGlobal Bangkok 2024.
- Bangkok figures reported by Blockscout included one giant mascot, 500 capybara plushies, 950 new community members, 259 winning hacker teams, and a $20,000 bounty pool.
- A Capybara runner game was later added to Blockscout's not-found block page. The player jumps over obstacles, and a score of 1,000+ was tied to a Merit reward.
- The October 2024 origin article explicitly said the mascot still had no name. I found no later official source that establishes “Scout” as its proper name. Official writing usually calls it “Capy,” “the Blockscout Capybara,” “our furry fren,” or the mascot.

## Visual DNA in the official images

- Rounded, very wide capybara silhouette with small ears and a large blunt muzzle.
- Warm orange-brown body, simple dark outline, dot/oval eyes, tiny paws.
- Purple or blue scout cap/hat and scout clothing.
- Blockscout's pixel-like symbol is commonly used as a badge, buckle, or chest mark.
- Expressions and poses are friendly, calm, slightly goofy, and non-aggressive.
- Recurring props and roles: hearts, merit badges, hooded hacker outfit, guide/scout uniform, sunglasses, plush toy, statue, and runner-game sprite.
- The original design journey moved away from a realistic animal toward a flat cartoon mascot suitable for stickers, NFTs, merch, and memes.

## Robinhood Chain context

- Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum-compatible Arbitrum Layer 2 built for onchain financial infrastructure, including tokenized real-world assets.
- Mainnet chain ID: `4663`.
- Native gas token: ETH.
- Official public explorer listed by Robinhood: `https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com`.
- The chain is permissionless, so anyone can deploy a token. A token being visible on the explorer does not mean Robinhood or Blockscout endorses it.
- Blockscout's August 2026 analysis reported that memes dominated Robinhood Chain trading activity in July, while the chain remained focused on RWAs. The article also stressed the counterfeit/lookalike-token problem and the need to verify exact contract addresses.

## Recommended narrative territory

The strongest story territory is “the friendly explorer of Robinhood Chain”:

- Born to explore, not to rule.
- Calm while the chart is chaotic.
- Guides the huddle through blocks, contracts, memes, and RWAs.
- Earns badges for surviving onchain adventures.
- Semi-aquatic adaptability becomes cross-chain adaptability.
- A scout who checks the contract twice and never gets lost in a sea of copycats.

This keeps the useful parts of the lore—exploration, huddles, badges, calmness, helpfulness—without falsely claiming the token is official.

## Name and voice direction

`KapiScout` gives the project an ownable character name while keeping the capybara/scout idea immediately legible. It should be presented as one word and one character—not as “Blockscout Capybara Coin.” Useful voice lines include “the friendliest explorer on Robinhood Chain,” “calm through every block,” and “the huddle always finds the way.”

Before launch, run another live token, domain, X handle, and trademark check. “Blockscout,” its logo, the exact mascot artwork, and Robinhood branding should not be placed in the final token logo without permission.

## IP and affiliation guardrail

Blockscout's published terms define its names, logos, trademarks, designs, drawings, and pictures as intellectual property and state that users receive no ownership rights except rights expressly granted in writing. The public media kit makes official assets accessible; it does not by itself grant a commercial derivative license for a memecoin.

Safest routes:

1. Ask Blockscout for written permission to use the exact mascot and brand marks.
2. If permission is unavailable, create a clearly original capybara explorer character: new silhouette, outfit, palette, insignia, name, and visual system; omit the Blockscout and Robinhood logos.
3. Label the project “unofficial” and never imply partnership, endorsement, or investment safety.
